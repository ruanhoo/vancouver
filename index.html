    Little tips for big adventures

    🌸

    When to go

    Cherry blossoms in spring, kayaks and paddleboards in summer, skiing at Whistler from winter. Bring a rain jacket in any season.

    🚇

    Getting around

    SkyTrain, buses and the SeaBus cover the city. For Whistler, Victoria and Hope, rent a car, take a ferry or board a train.

    ⛴️

    Ferries

    BC Ferries to Victoria and Nanaimo can sell out in summer. Reserve ahead for cars.

    🧥

    Dress

    Locals live in hoodies, yoga pants and shell jackets. Layers and comfy shoes win.
